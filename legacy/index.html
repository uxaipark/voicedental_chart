every field a complete chart must capture
FieldGranularityRange / valuesConvention & why it is recorded
Per site — six per tooth (MB · B · DB / ML · L · DL)
Gingival margin (GM)6 / tooth−5 … +12 mmMargin position relative to the CEJ. Positive = recession; negative = margin coronal to the CEJ (pseudo-pocket, overgrowth). Sign convention must be fixed before the exam.
Probing depth (PD)6 / tooth0 … 15 mmFree gingival margin to the base of the sulcus, UNC-15 probe, rounded up to the whole millimetre.
Attachment level (CAL)6 / toothcomputedRead-only: PD + GM. The measure that defines attachment loss and drives AAP staging — never typed by hand.
Bleeding on probing6 / toothyes / noScored within 30 s of probing. Full-mouth BOP % is the standard inflammation index; ≥ 10 % meets the gingivitis case definition.
Suppuration6 / toothyes / noExudate on pressure — marks an active lesion and raises urgency independently of depth.
Plaque6 / toothyes / noSupragingival biofilm; produces the plaque index (PI %) that anchors the home-care discussion.
Calculus6 / toothyes / noSub- or supragingival deposit felt on probing. Supports quadrant SRP justification (D4341 / D4342).
Gingival index6 / tooth0 · 1 · 2 · 3Löe & Silness. Optional, but the only field that separates colour/oedema change from frank bleeding.
MGJ / keratinized width1 / surface *0 … 12 mmGingival margin to mucogingival junction. Maxillary facial and mandibular facial + lingual. Under 2 mm flags a mucogingival defect.
Per tooth
Mobility1 / tooth0 · 1 · 2 · 3Miller scale, read from the facial. Class 3 (horizontal + vertical) is a stage-IV complexity factor.
Furcation1–3 / tooth0 · I · II · III · IVGlickman class, Nabers probe. Maxillary molars: buccal, mesiopalatal, distopalatal. Mandibular molars: buccal, lingual. Maxillary first premolars: mesial, distal.
Recession class1 / toothMiller I–IV · Cairo RT1–3Classifies the defect for root-coverage prognosis; Cairo grades it by interproximal attachment loss rather than by the MGJ.
Tooth status1 / toothpresent · missing · implantMissing teeth drop out of every index; implants are probed but diagnosed separately (peri-implant mucositis vs peri-implantitis).
Restoration flag1 / toothcrown · ponticCrown margins displace the CEJ reference; pontics carry no probing sites at all.
Tooth note1 / toothfree textFracture, mucogingival defect, endo-perio lesion, open contact — anything that changes the plan but has no numeric field.
Per exam
Exam metadata1 / examdate · provider · probe · sequenceRequired to compare against prior exams and to support the insurance narrative.
Indices1 / examcomputedMean PD, mean CAL, BOP %, PI %, and site counts ≥ 4 / ≥ 5 / ≥ 6 mm — the figures that show change over time.
AAP 2017 diagnosis1 / examStage I–IV · Grade A–C · extentStage from severity + complexity, grade from bone-loss/age ratio with smoking and HbA1c, extent from the share of involved sites (under 30 % = localized).

* MGJ is charted on maxillary facial and on mandibular facial and lingual surfaces — there is no maxillary palatal mucogingival junction to measure.

Sample chart: all 32 teeth charted, including partially erupted third molars and an implant at #19 — the stage, grade and extent shown are recomputed from whatever is on the chart. Demonstration data — not a patient record. Field set and layout compiled from the Open Dental perio chart specification, periodontalchart-online, and the AAP/EFP 2017 classification.
